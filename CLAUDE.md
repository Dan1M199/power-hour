# CLAUDE.md — The Power Hour

Context for Claude Code working in this repo. Read README.md for the user-facing overview.

## What this is

A shareable website for Dan's 12-team Sleeper fantasy league, **NFL Fantasy Championship** (league ID `1312001652454088704`, 2026 season). Leaguemates open it to see power rankings with explanations, a live waiver/trade feed, weekly matchup odds, and "nerd lab" stats. "The Power Hour" is also the name of Dan's league newsletter. Hosted on Vercel from GitHub; every push to the main branch deploys.

League facts the code relies on: half-PPR, 12 teams, starting slots QB, RB, RB, WR, WR, TE, FLEX, FLEX, K, DEF + 5 bench. Regular season ends week 14, playoffs start week 15, top 6 make it. Standings tiebreaker is points for. Waivers are rolling (no FAAB used in practice).

## Architecture

React, built with Vite (`vite.config.js`) into `dist/`, which Vercel builds (`npm ci`, `npm run build`) and serves (`vercel.json`: framework vite, output `dist`). Dependencies: `react` and `react-dom` (19), `html-to-image` (1.11.13, pinned, loaded only when someone shares the team dashboard), `framer-motion` and `lucide-react` (the headline reactions only, in their own lazy chunk), and for the build `vite` (8) and `@vitejs/plugin-react`. The pages are React components in `src/`, plain JSX (no TypeScript): the Live page (`src/live`: `LivePage` with `YourWeek`, the weekly list, projected standings and the final-scores view with its reveal button), the power rankings (`src/rankings`), the hero both share (`src/hero`: `Hero` puts the `Carousel` and page head together; from 1100px its `side` card, the you strip or Your week, stacks under the page head beside the carousel in `.hero-side`, its foot on the carousel's foot, and `hint` stands in when no team is picked; narrower, `side` follows the hero as before), the Wire (`src/wire`), Games (`src/games`), the Lab (`src/lab`: one page with three sections, personal to league-wide: Your team, the team dashboard from `src/dashboard`; Trades, the trade ideas from `src/trades`, once the Trade lab; League, the charts in `LabPage`, once the Nerd lab. `Lab.jsx` puts one heading over all three, `LabHead` in `parts.jsx`: the title, the section switcher `.lab-tabs` (a tray like the header's tabs, the active section on the amber pill, each segment with a line saying what's in it, such as the trade-idea count; phones show the labels only), the section's own line and, for Trades, the team picker on the right), the charts (`src/charts.jsx`) and the shared bits (`src/shared.jsx`). `app.js` is the frame: it loads the data, computes the league (`build`, `headlines`), writes the headlines as data (the stories, `carouselProps()`), keeps the site's state (`DATA`, `myTeam`, the pages' filters in `wireState`, `tradeState`, `labState`) and the props each page needs (`rankingsProps()`, `liveProps()`; `liveProps` renders `DATA.after` through `withData`), runs the router and the motion, and owns the two overlays that are still plain DOM (the important message, `maybeMessage`, and the final call's reveal, `passThenReveal`/`openReveal`). The pages were moved one at a time, each checked pixel-identical to the site before it. One React root sits on `#app` (`pageRoot` in `src/mount.jsx`): `route()` looks the tab up in `PAGES` (the page component and its props; `D`, `me` and `h` go to every page) and renders it synchronously (`flushSync`, so `reveal()` and view transitions see it) under a fresh key, so a page always mounts anew; what a page keeps when you leave it lives in `app.js`'s state objects and the stores. A page's filters are React state, kept in `app.js`'s state objects through `state`/`onState` (`wireState`, `tradeState`) so they survive leaving the page; the Lab's Trades picker (`TradePicker`, in the Lab's heading) sets your team for the whole site (`onTeam`, `setMyTeam`). Trade ideas are cached per team on the league (`D._ideas`). Games (`src/games`): `GamesPage` keeps which breakdowns are open (and which were ever built, kept for the fold) and does the desktop FLIP glide in a layout effect; `MatchupCard` has the odds toggle, `BetLine`, `Fans` and the `Breakdown` (`watchItem` became `Watch`, bolding names by splitting the text); `GameOfTheWeek` has the face-off and the playoff drive (its result is React state). Fan picks live in `src/games/votes.js` (`loadVotes`, called once at startup by `app.js`, `castVote`, and `useVotes` for React, through `useSyncExternalStore`). Rankings (`src/rankings`): `RankingsPage` keeps which rows are open (the jersey pin and the first-open nudge, `ph-opened-rank`, live in its `toggle`) and the you strip's jump; `RankRow` builds its `Report` (manager card, superlatives, reasons, radar and weekly bars through `Chart`, schedule, seeds) the first time it opens; `Poll` and `Futures` are their own components. The hero (`src/hero`): `Hero` is the `.hero`, holding the `Carousel` (props from `carouselProps()` in `app.js`: the stories, today's date, the hold note, night edition, section labels), the page head each page passes in (`RankHead`, `LiveHead`). The headlines are a layered ring (Dan's request Nov 10): Bencho's "Carousel" (bencho.dev, MIT, credited in the file), converted to JSX in `src/hero/Ring.jsx` with its comments kept (`useRing`: the turn, the drag with a flick, the settle; `spotOf`; `Card`, the give under a mouse). Each story is its own scoreboard card (`.car-slot` > `.car-float` > `.car-card` > `.slide`); `.lead-car` is only the frame: the hold note as a pill, the stack, then the controls on a board pill of their own (`.car-nav`). The resting places are a square deck, not the ring's fan (Dan found the side slivers at their own tilts jumbled, Nov 10): `spotOf` puts each card `d` places behind the front one a `step` higher (13px, 10 on phones) and 5% smaller, squarely behind, at most two showing (`SHOWN`), in shade (`--dim`); no resting angle, no sideways offset, no drift. On a swipe the front card is dealt off to the left clear of the deck (`reach`), tucks to the bottom's size and slides in underneath while the rest step forward; it swaps from the top of the paint order to the bottom at the far point, where it overlaps nothing. Cards are as wide as the frame and pinned by their top edge; a card behind comes forward when tapped. A press becomes a drag only after 8px sideways (the cards hold buttons; the click after a drag is swallowed; `.rxn` and inputs never start one), and a trackpad's sideways scroll turns it. `layout()` in `Carousel.jsx` measures the frame and writes `--side`, `--pad`, `--card-w` and `--card-h` (wide: every card as tall as the tallest, square at least) or `--front-h` (phones: the frame hugs the front story, the cards behind are cut to it, `data-fit`); it runs on a glide's start, real width changes, font load and any slide's size change. Slides behind are `inert`. The carousel runs itself: dots, arrows, swipe, the phone fit, the 60%-on-screen hold, the pause (kept for the visit) and the video headline (`useClip`, which also watches `visibilitychange` and `html.locked`). The news ticker (Nov 3) was removed at Dan's request (Nov 12); `tickOf(st).hook` still feeds the reaction bar's message pill, and the desk's Ticker fieldset still writes a story's `tick`. Headline reactions (Dan's request Nov 3): Bencho's "Emoji reactions" component (bencho.dev, MIT, credited in the file), converted to JSX in `src/hero/EmojiReactions.jsx` with its comments kept, sits as a bar under each slide's deck. The headline's attention catcher (`tickOf(st).hook` in `Carousel.jsx`) is the message pill that turns into the picker; the set is the league's (`SETS.League`: fire, haha, dead, clown, GOAT, looking; the KIND map gives the new ones an arrival); chips come from the server and every change goes out through `onReact`, sent once a flown reaction lands (at once under reduced motion); a first tap on the add button always opens the tray: on touch the browser aims the tap's own click at whatever is under the finger after the pill opens (an emoji), so the button swallows the one click after its touch (`swallowClick`), `along` checks the finger is on the pill vertically as well as across, and a hold that never slides onto the pill leaves it open instead of picking; the carousel holds while the picker is open (`.lead-car.reacting`) and re-fits on any slide's size change (a ResizeObserver, since the bar arrives after the first paint). It's `React.lazy`, so framer-motion only downloads when `/api/reactions` says the store is on; the demo and preview have no bar. Bencho's tokens are mapped on `.rxn` itself to the scoreboard's (body font, chalk ink, the cast rows' 8% white, yellow as the signal since yellow means you); its dark-theme overrides are dropped because the board is dark in both themes, and its tooltip is 11.5px. State is `src/hero/reactions.js` (`rx`, `useReactions`, `loadReactions(keys)`, `react(key, e, on)`, optimistic with a rollback). `api/reactions.js`: keys are the desk's (`storyKeys`, `w{week}:{type}`), each (key, emoji) a Redis set of device hashes (`ph:rx:{key}:{emoji}`, 300-day TTL), so one voice per device per emoji; the device is the desk's `ph_anon` cookie; `MAX_PER_HOUR` 300 changes per network; GET `?keys=` (up to 12) returns `{enabled, set, counts, mine}`, POST `{key, emoji, on}`; without the store GET says `{enabled: false}`. `scripts/test-reactions.mjs` covers it. The important message's replay chip asks `message.ready()` (the HEAD check) and plays through `maybeMessage(true)`. The fan poll's state is `pollStore` (`createStore` in `src/store.js`), set by `loadPoll`/`castPoll` in `app.js`. The ranking rows' entrance and count-ups (`revealRanks`) still run on the React-rendered rows. Helpers the Games page needs from `app.js`: `american`, `half`, `isChamp`/`champSeason` (for `ChampTag`/`ChampBadge` in `src/shared.jsx`), `bull` (`bullInfo`, the money week chip as data) and `moneyWeek`. Formatting and names come from `app.js` as one helpers object (`reactHelpers`: `f1`, `pct`, `ordinal`, `short`, `record`, `first`, `groupSaid`, `pctW`, `nameOf`, `posOf`, `faceSrc`, `nfl`, `shortName`, `teams`), so the React pages read exactly like the rest. Charts are components in `src/charts.jsx` (`BumpChart`, `Scatter`, `Radar`, `WeeklyBars`, `DivergingBars`, `HeatTable`). Pictures in React are `TeamAvatar` (the Sleeper team picture) and `Headshot` (the manager's photo), also in `src/shared.jsx`; each handles its own load error, so never give a React image `data-initials`. Keep a space that sits between words in the same string as them (`{`${name} `}`), not in a text node of its own: separate text nodes kern a hair differently. The pure modules in `lib/` (engine, stories, matchup, trades, labmodels, nightdesk, bullseye, dashboard numbers, newsclock, members) stay plain JS and are imported by React and the API alike; keep them free of DOM and React. The build only bundles, minifies and fingerprints: nothing is translated for older browsers (`target`/`cssTarget` esnext), small files aren't inlined (`assetsInlineLimit: 0`), bundled files land in `dist/bundle/` with hashes in their names, and files the page asks for by name at runtime are copied as they are (`assets/`, `demo/`). `api/` and the `lib/` modules it imports are built by Vercel as functions, as before. Each step was checked pixel-identical to the site before it on every page, desktop and phone. Only `dist/` is public (the repo's own files aren't).

- `index.html`, `styles.css`, `app.js` — the site. The browser never calls the Sleeper API: it loads `/api/league?d=KEY` and `/api/ros?d=KEY`, where KEY is the Central date rolling over at 6am (`morningKey()`), so each is fetched once per morning and shared from the CDN (and kept an hour by the browser, `max-age=3600`: a key's snapshot never changes). Load order: the `<head>` preloads both Latin font files and the stylesheet, and Vite adds the bundle's module script and a modulepreload for each chunk. A plain inline `<script data-early>` dynamically imports `/lib/newsclock.js` (the build points it at its own small chunk) and starts both data requests while the main bundle downloads (`window.__early`, skipped in the demo); `loadLive()` reuses them through `early()` when the URL still matches and sets `window.__early` itself first if they never started, so a request is never made twice.
- `api/league.js` — the morning Sleeper snapshot: state, league, users, rosters, matchups weeks 1–14, transactions weeks 1–current, plus `fetched`. It only answers the current key from `leagueKey()` (any other `?d=` gets a 307 to it) and keeps each snapshot in the store (`once('league', key)`, 3 days), so a deploy (Vercel empties the CDN) never re-reads Sleeper. `X-Snapshot` says `store` or `live`. `max-age=3600, s-maxage=86400`; errors are `no-store`. Also exports `snapshot()` for scripts. Hash routing: `#live` (only during a live week), `#rankings`, `#admin` (the headline desk, no tab), `#wire`, `#matchups`, `#lab/team`, `#lab/trades`, `#lab/league` (`route()` splits the hash on `/` into the view and `labState.sub`; plain `#lab` opens the last section this visit, team at first, and the old `#trades` opens `#lab/trades`; either way `history.replaceState` writes the full address, so no extra history entry or hashchange).
- `api/ros.js` — Vercel serverless function (Node 18+, global `fetch`). Scrapes the three ROS sources, fetches Sleeper's `/players/nfl` (~5 MB), matches players to Sleeper IDs, returns `{ updated, sources, weights, players: {id: {n,p,t,i,a}}, values: {id: {v, r, spread, pos, wk, fl, cl, ir, sos, g, rk, prk, wkEst?}}, byes: {TEAM: week}, weekly: {week, sources, proj: {id: [pts, fp, sl]}} }`. Bye weeks come from FantasyPros (`player_bye_week` on every ecrData row). Only the current `morningKey()` is served (others redirect). Sleeper's player map is slimmed to fantasy positions and stored on its own (`once('players', key)`), so it's read at most once per morning; the built body is stored for 3 days, or 1h when a ranking source failed (the retry re-scrapes the rankings and reuses the stored players). Edge-cached 24h (`s-maxage=86400`) and an hour in the browser (`max-age=3600`); if a source failed, 1h at the edge and not at all in the browser. No cron; the only database is the fan-picks store behind `api/votes.js`.
- `lib/weekly.js` — this week's player projections for the matchups, pure: the plain average of FantasyPros' API (`/public/v2/json/nfl/{season}/projections`, `points_half`, env `FANTASYPROS_API_KEY`) and Sleeper's projections endpoint (`api.sleeper.app/projections/nfl/{season}/{week}`, `pts_half_ppr`), whichever has the player. `api/ros.js` fetches both for `nflWeek(morningKey)` (weeks turn over Tuesday; `SEASON_TUESDAY` needs updating each season) and stores each on its own (`once('fpproj'|'slproj', key)`), so it's one Sleeper call and about 23 FantasyPros calls per morning. FantasyPros' free tier returns 10 players a call and answers bursts with 429, so `fpIdsToAsk` picks the likely starters from its ROS ranking (`FP_DEPTH`, by `fpid` from ecrData), one call every `FP_GAP_MS`, with retries. Without the API key it runs on Sleeper alone (no hourly retry). The engine uses `weekly` only when `weekly.week` is the upcoming week: `thisWeekProj` reads it (DraftSharks' ROS `wk` for a player neither has), `muWeek[upcoming]` and the live rest are the projection itself (no scale or points-per-game blend), and each preview's `projA/projB` is `t.setProj`, the set lineup's projection (the best lineup if none is set), the same total `breakdown()` adds up. Floors and ceilings are DraftSharks' scaled by this week's projection over `wk`. `meta.hasWeekly` switches the Games copy.
- `lib/newsclock.js` — the shared clock (page and API): `TZ`, `NEWS_CLOCK` (hold, late pull, night edition, game window), `morningKey`, `leagueKey`, `isOnHold`, `isNightDesk`, `inGameWindow`. The early data script in `index.html` imports it on its own (the build gives it a small chunk), so keep it free of imports.
- `lib/snapstore.js` — `once(name, key, produce)`: each snapshot kept gzipped in the Upstash store (same env vars as the fan picks) under its clock key, with a lock so simultaneous first visitors share one pull. Without the env vars it falls back to pulling on each CDN miss. `scripts/test-snapshots.mjs` counts Sleeper calls.
- `lib/sources.js` — regex-only parsers (must run in Node without a DOM):
  - FantasyPros: `var ecrData = {...};` embedded in the page HTML (~400 players incl. K/DST).
  - DraftSharks: the page only server-renders 25 rows; the full list is `GET /ros-rankings/load-rows?offset=0&limit=600&...` (HTML `<tr class="player-row">` fragments, includes IDP, re-ranked within offense). Also provides weekly proj pts, floor, ceiling, injury risk %, SOS, projected games.
  - LeagueLogs: server-rendered `<table>`, 200 players incl. DST, no K. Its order nearly mirrors FantasyPros ECR, so it is weighted 0.5.
- `lib/consensus.js` — `normName` + `NICKNAMES` map, `buildIndex(sleeperPlayers)`, `matchRow`, `buildConsensus`. Rank → value is `100 * exp(-0.0115 * (rank-1))`, weighted average across sources (`SOURCE_WEIGHTS`). Missing weekly projections are filled by a per-position linear fit of DraftSharks pts vs value, falling back to `DEFAULT_FIT` if DraftSharks is down.
- `lib/engine.js` — `computeLeague(input)`: pure, no DOM, runs in browser and Node. Produces teams (sorted by power rank), weekly results, all-play, luck, efficiency, ROS best lineup (greedy fixed-then-flex), position-group ranks (`groupScores`, quality over quantity: each starter's weekly projection minus the league baseline at his position, `meta.posBase`, the Nth-best rostered player with N = teams × dedicated slots, so WR24, RB24, QB12, TE12; dedicated slots weigh `GROUP_W.fixed` 1.25 and can go negative, flex use weighs 0.5 then 0.25 and never below zero, so a pile of middling receivers can't outrank a better group one player shorter; the Trade lab scores groups the same way, and tooltips read "+x pts/wk over the WR24 line" via `groupSaid` in `app.js`), power score + rank history, Monte Carlo playoff odds and championship odds (10k sims, seeded; the playoff bracket is simulated on Sleeper's fixed 4/6/8-team bracket, giving `champPct`/`finalPct`; `simulate` runs on team indexes and typed arrays but draws the same random numbers in the same order as the plain version it replaced, and `scripts/test-engine.mjs` checks a fingerprint of the fixture's odds, so a change to it that shifts any probability fails the test), matchup previews, explanations (`explain`), tiers (`tierFor`), transaction feed with ROS value deltas (trades priced over replacement: each net extra player received costs `meta.replacement`, the mean value of the top 3 free agents, so consolidation trades are not penalised; waiver moves stay raw), best available free agents, "divisive" players.
- `lib/stories.js` — the newsroom, pure like the engine. `buildStories(D, {now, dateKey})` scores candidate stories (fresh trades and wire moves, unbeaten/winless/streaks, last week's upsets/blowouts/nail-biters, rank moves, luck, game of the week) and returns `{lead, more}`; a date-seeded jitter rotates near-ties daily. `keyMatchup(D)` picks the game of the week by 0.55 × stakes + 0.45 × closeness, where stakes is the combined playoff-odds swing (`playoffIfWin − playoffIfLoss` from the simulation). `matchupQuips(D)` gives one data-driven one-liner per matchup, no repeated joke within a week.
- `api/votes.js` — fan picks. GET `?week=&voter=` returns `{week, open, counts: {"3-8": {"3": n}}, mine}`; POST `{week, pair, pick, voter}` records a vote (re-voting replaces the device's pick). Storage is Upstash Redis over its REST pipeline API with plain fetch (env `KV_REST_API_URL`/`KV_REST_API_TOKEN` or `UPSTASH_REDIS_REST_*`); without them GET returns `{enabled: false}` (200, no console noise), POST returns 503, and the UI hides voting. Server validates against the cached `/api/league` snapshot on its own host (current week, real pair, pick in pair), never Sleeper directly, and closes voting from Thursday 7:15pm Central through Monday or once the snapshot shows points. `VOTES_NOW` env overrides the clock in tests. Soft cap of 3 voters per hashed IP per matchup. Keys `ph:votes:{week}:{pair}` (hash voter→pick) and `ph:net:{week}:{pair}:{iphash}` (set), 120-day TTL. The client keeps its voter id in `localStorage` `ph-voter` and hides the split until you've voted.
- `api/admin-auth.js` — private headline-editor sign-in; verifies `ADMIN_PASSWORD_HASH` or `ADMIN_KEY`, sets the HttpOnly `ph_admin` cookie and stores its session in `ph:admin`.
- The headline desk (`#admin`, Dan's request Nov 6): Dan's own portal for posting headlines without a code push. `api/headlines.js` stores posts in one Upstash hash (`ph:hl:posts`, 300-day TTL); GET (`no-store`) returns `{enabled, admin, now, posts}` (live ones by `order`; Dan also gets `old`: taken down or run out in the last 14 days), POST `post`/`edit`/`remove`/`restore`/`order` is Dan only: `whoAmI` from `api/admin-auth.js` must be `ADMIN`, so signing in uses the editor password or `ADMIN_KEY`. `cleanStory` trims a post to the carousel's story shape (kicker, stamp, tone, group, breaking, headline, deck, tick, rids, player with `pin: {rid, label}`, mark kept only if it's in the text, tx ids, week, `until` or null). The text is kept as written. Dan can also rewrite or hide the site's own headlines (Nov 6): POST `site-edit` {key, story} / `site-hide` {key} / `site-reset` {key}, keyed by the desk key (`w{week}:{type}`, so it covers that week only), stored in `ph:hl:site` and dropped after 30 days; GET returns them to everyone as `site`, and `carouselProps()` applies them (`siteStories()` gives each generated story its `key` first, `hydrate()` turns a stored story into a slide; `carouselProps({ all: true })` keeps the hidden ones, marked, for the desk). `storyKeys` honours `st.key`, so sorting into sections never moves a key. Every headline sits in a section (Dan's request Nov 6): `GROUPS` (Trades, Rumors, Wire, Games, Players, League), a story's own `group` or `TYPE_GROUP` by type (League otherwise), and `bySection` gathers the carousel into sections in the order they first come up, so the lead stays first and the dots group on every page. `scripts/test-headlines.mjs` covers it. Client state is `src/admin/posts.js` (`posts`, `usePosts`, `loadPosts`, `postCall`); `main()` in `app.js` starts `loadPosts()` beside the data (skipped in the demo), waits at most 1.2 s, and re-routes if posts land later. `carouselProps()` puts `postedStories()` (type `p-{id}`, so reactions and desk tags are per post; teams and the pin looked up in today's league; expired ones dropped by the page clock) ahead of the generated stories on the rankings and Live pages. `src/admin/AdminPage.jsx` (its own chunk through `AdminShell.jsx`, mounted from `PAGES.admin`, no tab): the password gate for anyone not admin (Dan's request Nov 6: `#admin` is password protected; the box sends `password` to `api/admin-auth.js`, which checks it against the salted scrypt hash in `ADMIN_PASSWORD_HASH` and signs the device in as Dan for a year; wrong guesses are capped at `MAX_FAILS`, 8 an hour per network; the optional `ADMIN_KEY` still works in the same box; `node scripts/admin-password.mjs <new>` prints a new hash); for Dan, one desk top to bottom. The running order (`.adm-order`): his posts numbered on the grass like yard numbers (yellow, half under the card), with reorder arrows, Edit and Take down, then the site's own headlines (`site` prop, from `carouselProps()`) on flat trays, greyed; the newest or just-edited post wears the ring and gold ledge (`.fresh`). ### Power score

One formula for everyone: `WEIGHTS` in `lib/engine.js`, in Dan's priority order. Standing 40% (`standing` 0.31, place in the real standings: wins then points for; `winPct` 0.09), scoring 28% (`pfPerGame` 0.20, `allPlay` 0.04, `form` 0.04), schedule left 17% (`sos` 0.17), rest-of-season roster 15% (`rosProj` 0.13, `depth` 0.02). `PARTS` groups them; each team gets `t.parts` (standing, scoring, schedule, roster sub-scores) shown as the four bars on a ranking row, and `t.seed` (standings place). There is no admin page or published config: the page never passes weights, so every device computes the same ranks from the same snapshot (`computeLeague({ weights })` is only for scripts). Each metric is z-scored across the 12 teams; score = `50 + 22 * weighted z`, clamped 1–99. `sos` is schedule wins (`t.schedWins`): sum over the remaining regular-season games of P(win vs actual opponent) − P(win vs average opponent), using the same `projMean` and pooled SD as the playoff simulation, so the projection block runs before the power score. The engine also still returns `rosScore`, `sosScore`, `resultsScore`. Bye weeks (`byes` input from `/api/ros`) count everywhere: `weekProj(id, w)` is zero on a player's bye (and this week if ruled out); each team's `weekProj[w]` is its best lineup that week; `rosProj` is their regular-season average; `muWeek[w]` (week-specific strength) drives schedule wins and the simulation, so drawing a team in its bye-heavy week counts as easier. `effWeekly` (lineup display, position groups, Trade lab) is the per-game projection times health times the share of remaining regular-season weeks not lost to a bye. If `/api/ros` fails, ROS terms are dropped (schedule stays, using points per game) and the page shows a "results-only mode" notice. Rank history holds the ROS and schedule terms at today's value, so week-to-week movement comes from results only.

### Week in progress

After Thursday night the current week is partly played. An NFL team counts as played once any rostered player of theirs has a non-zero score in `matchups[upcoming]`. In `computeLeague`: `liveProj(id)` is actual points for played players, else `thisWeekProj`; each team gets `t.live = {banked, rest, left, played[]}` (locked starters' points, best lineup of not-yet-played players in the open slots, share of slots still open). `muWeek[upcoming]` becomes banked plus the blended rest, and `sdWeek[upcoming] = sd * sqrt(left)`, which drives the previews' win odds and the simulation (playoff and title odds). Previews carry `liveA/liveB`. `D.live = {week, teams}`. Season PF and records stay Sleeper's official numbers until the week is scored. The matchup breakdown tags played players FINAL and never suggests swapping them. `buildStories` adds a `dud` and a `star` story from the played games; the `editor` option (`EDITOR` in `app.js`, Dan) makes his own underperforming starter the dud and the lead. Hand-written headlines live in `PUNS` in `lib/stories.js`.

## Design system

Concept: the bowl, a day game seen as a stadium. A pill is geometrically a stadium, so every surface is one (or a circle), shaded like a real rounded thing and stacked in tiers. Three rules, set out in the "the bowl" section of `styles.css` (right before the night game section, so night overrides still win): nothing has a sharp corner; things come from behind other things; everything is shaded. Two themes: the day game (light) and the night game (dark), the same stadium after sunset. Every colour is a `:root` token (`--paper` for white chip and input surfaces, since `--chalk` stays literal white for text on the scoreboards; `--header`, `--good-bg`, `--bad-bg`, `--info-bg`, `--hover`, `--notice-bg`, `--mine-edge`, `--drawer`, `--in-*`), redefined under `:root[data-theme='dark']` in the night game section at the end of `styles.css`, which also holds the few one-off night overrides (the starry sky, the dimmer chalk, chart plates and halos, the tooltip) and the dashboard's own night palette. ## Gotchas

- Sleeper roster `fpts` etc. are split into integer + `_decimal`/100.
- Sleeper team defenses use the team abbreviation as player ID (`"DET"`). Ranking sites spell some teams differently; `TEAM_FIX` in `sources.js` normalises (JAC→JAX, WSH→WAS…).
- Players the sources haven't listed yet (fresh signings) project 0. Patch them in `OVERRIDES` in `lib/overrides.js` (`{pos, wk}`); `app.js` applies it before `computeLeague`, only while the consensus has no `wk` for that player, and the footer lists active patches. Remove entries once the sources catch up.
- Name mismatches go in `NICKNAMES` (e.g. Bam Knight → Zonovan Knight, Cameron Skattebo → Cam Skattebo). Check `/api/ros` → `sources.*.matched` after changes; ~190 of 194 rostered players should have values.
- Scrapers may be blocked from Vercel IPs (browser-like User-Agent is set). A failed source degrades gracefully; don't make the whole endpoint fail on one source.
- Sleeper asks for `/players/nfl` at most once a day; only `api/ros.js` calls it, and the edge cache enforces that. Never call it from the browser.
- Sleeper is called only by `api/league.js` and `api/ros.js`, on the clock: once per morning (for ros: the player map and, approved by Dan, this week's projections), plus the Sunday late pull and the Monday final pull for the league. Dan does not want extra pulls: not on deploys, not for testing, not from new features. Keep reads going through `once()` with a clock key, never add browser calls to Sleeper, and ask Dan before adding any new Sleeper read or pull time. Testing uses the demo snapshot, fixtures and `?at=` (which fakes the page clock only; data keys follow the real clock).
- Ranking data belongs to FantasyPros, DraftSharks and LeagueLogs; this is a personal, non-commercial league site. Keep the attribution in the footer.
- The footer sponsor block is intentional: Dimassi's (struck through, contract expired) and the current sponsor TTP (Texas Truck Parts) with `assets/ttp-logo.webp` (144px, 5 KB, lazy and low priority; re-encoded in headless Chromium from the master `assets/ttp-logo.png`, 84 KB, which the page never loads). The source logo's "TT" letters were transparent knockouts; the PNG has them filled white so they show on the dark background. `bundle-preview.mjs` inlines the logo as a data URI.
- Anything tappable to expand uses the shared `XP` circle in `app.js` (`.xp` in CSS), which flips when its parent is open.
