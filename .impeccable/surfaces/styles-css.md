# The whole site

Scope: every page and overlay of The Power Hour. Visitor mode: Operate (managers check where they stand, mostly on phones from a group chat link). Build path: code-led; the direction was pinned by Dan, so no concept roll.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site reads as one flat sheet of dashboard tiles standing on a football field. It refuses the cartoon-glass league site: no gloss, sheen, glint or condensed stadium lettering.

OWN-WORLD: Sky bands, clouds and mowed stripes as the ground. Flat bone tiles (#FBF6EF) with a 2px dark green ink edge and a hard ledge, warm wells inside them. Big thin Geist (300) for names and numbers, Geist Mono captions, Inter labels with a dot. Amber for actions, violet (#6A4F9C) for your team, dark green boards for headlines and game of the week.

STORY: A manager arrives from the chat, reads their rank and odds at a glance as large numerals, opens a tile for the reasons, and leaves with something to argue about.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Header tile with logo, tabs on a well, active tab on the amber pill, theme button, team picker. Live: dark headline board left, big thin page title and desk right. Rankings: big thin title on the sky, then rows on the grass with giant thin painted ranks beside each tile, score at 46 to 58px.

FORM: Pinned by the user (the team dashboard's flat tiles and lettering, on the existing sky and field; keep painted rank numbers, dark boards, tilted prints; your team moves from yellow to violet). seed: none (pinned direction, no roll). Signature move: the painted rank numbers, redrawn thin and flat.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
